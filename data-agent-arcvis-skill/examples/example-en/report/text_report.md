# Monthly Music-Retail Sales Report (Text Report Requirements)

## Report Structure (three chapters, fixed order)

### Chapter 1 Overall Sales Performance

Required materials in this chapter:

- Monthly total sales and sales MoM growth rate (numbers + up/down verdict) — source: objective_1/G2

### Chapter 2 Channel-Mix Analysis

Required materials in this chapter:

- Channel sales distribution table (Channel / Sales Qty / Revenue / Sales Share, the full set of three channels) — source: objective_1/G1
- Per-channel performance conclusions (magnitude + MoM direction, citing concrete numbers) — source: objective_1/G3
- Channel sales distribution horizontal bar chart (spec in objective_1 chart methodology) — source: objective_1/G1

### Chapter 3 Key-Track Target Diagnosis

Required materials in this chapter:

- Track achievement detail table (Track ID / Track Name / Monthly Sales / Monthly Target / Achievement Rate / Verdict, achievement rate ascending) — source: objective_2/G1
- Overall achievement conclusion (overall achievement rate + unmet count and share) — source: objective_2/G2

## Tone and Style

- Executive-briefing tone: conclusions first, numbers as support, one-sentence conclusion opening each chapter;
- Number precision: sales as integers (copies), revenue to 2 decimal places (CNY), shares and achievement rates as percentages to 2 decimal places (e.g. 87.50%);
- Fixed verdict vocabulary: Met / Missed / — (missing target); vague phrasings like "basically met" or "close to target" are forbidden.

## Format Template

- Each chapter starts with "## Chapter N {Title}", followed by a one-sentence conclusion paragraph, then the material tables and elaboration;
- Table headers match the material-list column names verbatim; the only chart is the channel distribution in Chapter 2, spec in objective_1 chart methodology;
- Charts / tables / conclusions beyond the material list are forbidden.
